The Quest Has Just Begun

You are a knight, over the years you have persevered through thousands of trials. But now you have no quests left, at least that is what you thought until you encounter a wizard that has an offer that you simply cannot refuse. For you see, throughout your travels you have spent all your earned riches on buying food from local taverns. Alas, you possess no money, even more simply put, you are broke. So when the wizard offers 1,000 coins to find their pet hamster you can't refuse.
